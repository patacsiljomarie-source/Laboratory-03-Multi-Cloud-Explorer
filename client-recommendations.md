# Client Recommendations

## Client A – Startup Company
**Recommended Platform: Google Cloud Platform (GCP)**

GCP is a strong fit for a startup with a limited budget but expectations of rapid growth. GCP offers competitive, flexible pricing with per-second billing and sustained-use discounts, which helps keep costs low in the early stages. Its serverless and managed services also reduce the need for a large technical team to maintain infrastructure, letting the startup focus on building its product. As the company grows, GCP's global network and auto-scaling compute services can scale seamlessly to handle increased demand.

**Services the client could use:**
- **Compute Engine** – for flexible, scalable virtual machines
- **Cloud Run** – for deploying containerized applications without managing servers
- **Firestore/Cloud Storage** – for cost-effective, scalable data storage

## Client B – University
**Recommended Platform: Microsoft Azure**

Since the university already relies on Windows Server, Microsoft 365, and Active Directory, Azure is the most logical choice because it integrates natively with these existing systems. Migrating to Azure minimizes disruption, as identity management can extend directly from the university's current Active Directory setup. Azure also offers education-focused pricing and compliance certifications that are well-suited to academic institutions. This reduces both migration complexity and long-term licensing costs.

**Services the client could use:**
- **Azure Active Directory (Microsoft Entra ID)** – to extend existing identity management to the cloud
- **Azure Virtual Machines** – to migrate on-premises servers
- **Azure SQL Database** – to modernize database systems used by university applications

## Client C – AI Research Company
**Recommended Platform: Google Cloud Platform (GCP)**

GCP is the top choice for an AI research company because Google is a leader in artificial intelligence and machine learning infrastructure. GCP provides access to powerful GPUs and TPUs (Tensor Processing Units) specifically designed to accelerate AI model training and high-performance computing workloads. Its tools like Vertex AI and BigQuery also simplify the process of building, training, and analyzing large datasets for machine learning. This makes GCP the most technically aligned platform for AI-focused workloads.

**Services the client could use:**
- **Vertex AI** – for building, training, and deploying machine learning models
- **Compute Engine (with GPU/TPU support)** – for high-performance computing
- **BigQuery** – for large-scale data analysis to support AI research

## Client D – Global E-Commerce Company
**Recommended Platform: Amazon Web Services (AWS)**

AWS is the best fit for a multinational e-commerce company because of its extensive global infrastructure and proven track record in handling large-scale, high-traffic workloads. AWS has the most regions and availability zones of any provider in key global markets, ensuring low latency for customers worldwide. Its auto-scaling and load-balancing services allow the platform to automatically handle traffic surges, such as during sales events, without downtime. AWS's maturity and reliability also make it a trusted choice for mission-critical, always-available systems.

**Services the client could use:**
- **Amazon EC2 with Auto Scaling** – to automatically adjust compute capacity based on traffic
- **Elastic Load Balancing (ELB)** – to distribute traffic across multiple servers for high availability
- **Amazon CloudFront** – to deliver content quickly to customers around the world via its CDN
